# University and open-course lead list by country

Candidate flagship universities and open-courseware / MOOC platforms for future
country tranches. These are **research leads only** — none of these URLs are
verified against the project quality policy yet. Each candidate must still pass
first-party provenance, free-access, English-facing metadata, substantive-content,
and deduplication checks before any catalog record is created.

Coverage: 100 countries (superset of the top-100 population roster; several
entries are outside that roster). The final section lists cross-country resources
that are specifically strong for questions, PYQs, and assignments.

## Usage notes

- Several entries overlap with existing catalog records (e.g., MIT OCW, NPTEL,
  GATE, Cambridge past papers); deduplicate before promoting anything.
- Some leads are national MOOC platforms spanning many universities (FUN MOOC,
  K-MOOC, NAVOICA, XuetangX, Edraak, NAU, imoox) rather than single-institution
  sources; attribute records to the owning institution where identifiable.
- Countries in this list but not on the top-100 population roster (Armenia,
  Bahrain, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Brunei, Bulgaria, Costa Rica, Croatia, Cyprus,
  Estonia, Georgia, Iceland, Kuwait, Kyrgyzstan, Latvia, Lebanon, Lithuania,
  Luxembourg, Malta, Oman, Panama, Paraguay, Qatar, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia,
  Taiwan) remain lower-priority unless a tranche needs regional balance.
